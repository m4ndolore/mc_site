# Learn, Contact, Adoption, Unlearn, Launch again

Status: captured doctrine. Public page uses the campaign line. A public loop graphic is not shipped yet.

This is Merge Combinator's operating loop. Same job as Build-Measure-Learn, different test. Lean Startup measures whether a product is learning. This loop measures whether a capability is making contact with reality and earning adoption.

## Campaign

> Meet reality. Earn adoption. Unlearn the rest.

Use this as public language. It is on `/learn` as the hero supporting line.

## Operating line

> Build what gets adopted. Keep only what solves the core problem. Launch again.

Use this under the campaign line. It names the work: adoption, deletion, another attempt.

## Doctrine

```
Learn → Contact → Adoption → Unlearn → Launch again
          ↑                          ↓
          └─────── Momentum ─────────┘
```

- **Learn:** Acquire enough of the map to act.
- **Contact:** Test the map against operators, constraints, and live demand.
- **Adoption:** Look for behavioral proof, not compliments, pitch scores, or funding alone.
- **Unlearn:** Delete assumptions, requirements, and processes the evidence no longer supports.
- **Launch again:** Return with a simpler, better-aligned attempt.

**Momentum** is not a sixth step. It is the public record of contact and adoption, and the reason to enter the loop or re-enter it.

The Musk algorithm is the method of Unlearning: question, delete, simplify, accelerate, automate. Use it in that order. Do not automate what should have been deleted.
