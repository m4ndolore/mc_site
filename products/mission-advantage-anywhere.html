01

Product overview

Mission Advantage Anywhere is a deployable communications and reporting kit for continuity of operations while the mission and the network adapt. Teams disperse to austere and contingency locations, relocate a command post, or operate outside the base network boundary, and for the first 24 to 72 hours they are limited to what one person can carry. The kit is what that person carries.

It is delivered in three node classes that share one software layer and one data model. A Mobile node puts a single operator on the picture. A Flyaway node moves a team with a local datalink and satellite reach-back. A Persist node stays and becomes the head-end for the local net. Any combination of them forms a working network, and every node routes for every other node, so the net survives losing any one of them, including the one holding reach-back.

The kit is additive by design. Existing systems remain the authority. The kit carries traffic to them and reports the mission around itself while it does.

02

Key features

  • Three node classes, one software layer. Mobile, Flyaway and Persist scale from one operator to a standing site without changing how data reaches the C2 picture.
  • PACE is part of the design. Primary, alternate, contingency and emergency paths are set before the mission. The kit reports which path is carrying traffic and when that changed.
  • Peer-to-peer local net. Every node routes for every other node. No hub to lose.
  • It reports itself. System and mission telemetry through Dead Reckon; human observation through Anymouse. The kit is instrumented, so exercises produce data instead of anecdote.
  • Rides the systems already in place. Position and traffic land in TAK; telemetry and reporting feed Envision, MSS and Advana.
  • Commercial and replaceable. Components are commercial off the shelf and individually substitutable. No single vendor is load-bearing.
  • Carried, not operated, in flight. Devices stay powered off in the cockpit and all use is off-aircraft. No cockpit-use approval is required and nothing in the design depends on one.
  • An accreditation path, stated honestly. Components have IL4 precedent and the managed service supports CUI today. The specific combination is new and carries its own review.

Applications

Command post continuity of operations

The kit gives a relocating command post a working net and a reporting path before permanent communications exist.

Use case: A wing command post displaces to an alternate site. A Persist node establishes the local net and reach-back within the first hours; Mobile nodes keep dispersed staff on the picture while the primary path is rebuilt.

Dispersed beddown, first 72 hours

Aircrew and maintainers arrive at a contingency location with what one person can carry and still need to report.

Use case: A team beds down at an austere airfield outside the base network boundary. Flyaway nodes carry team traffic locally and push status and position to the C2 picture until the site is connected properly.

Flight line coordination and notification

Where coordination between stakeholders runs on personal cell phones and legacy voice, the kit closes the notification gap.

Use case: An event on the flight line requires an immediate coordinated response across organisations that do not share a net. The kit puts the same picture in front of all of them instead of relying on a chain of phone calls.

Personnel and asset accountability

Position and status reporting from people and equipment that would otherwise be tracked by spreadsheet and phone call.

Use case: During a dispersal or a domestic response, tracking of aircraft, equipment and personnel is currently manual and degrades under stress. Nodes report continuously into the systems the command post already uses.

Exercise instrumentation

The kit records what happened, so a rehearsal produces measured data rather than recollection.

Use case: During a major exercise, Dead Reckon records path state and system events while Anymouse captures what operators observed. The after-action product is a timeline, not a memory.

Defence support to civil authorities

A commercial, unclassified kit that works alongside civil agencies without requiring them to join a military network.

Use case: During a wildfire or storm response, guard teams operating with state and county partners share position and status on an unclassified picture that all participants can see.

Security and accreditation posture

Stated plainly, because the answer is not "already certified" and a partner deserves to know that before an exercise rather than during one.

  • Components. The kit adapts established IL4 components. Several are under active review by the USAF operational test community toward IL-6, and the managed service supports CUI today.
  • The combination is new. This specific hardware and software combination, in this use case and configuration, has not been reviewed or approved before. No authorisation is inherited and none is claimed.
  • Device management. Devices enrol under an existing electronic flight bag mobile device management programme rather than a new one.
  • Spectrum. Frequency authorisation for the datalink is an open item and a named engineering task, not an assumption.
  • Classified operation. The baseline is unclassified. SECRET operation is scoped and priced separately after discovery. Start unclassified, arrive classified.

Value proposition

Why Mission Advantage Anywhere. Communications planning fails in a predictable place: the stretch between arriving somewhere and being connected there. Dispersal, mission command under degraded conditions and distributed control all assume that stretch has already been covered. Covering it in a form one person can carry onto an aircraft is the job this kit does.

It bridges the gap

Continuity of operations while the mission and the network adapt, then a clean handover to whatever the site is eventually wired with.

It is additive

No rip and replace, no new command and control authority. Existing systems stay the authority and the kit feeds them.

It produces evidence

Two measured numbers: time to restore the net when the primary path drops, and time from an edge observation to its appearance on the C2 picture.

It starts small

A single node is a real capability and a real test. Scaling from there is a quantity decision.

13

What it is not

  • Not a command and control system, and not a new C2 authority.
  • Not a counter-UAS system. It does not detect and it does not defeat.
  • Not a replacement for tactical radios or for a fielded programme of record.
  • Not accredited for classified operation today.
  • Not certified for cockpit use. It is carried powered off and used off-aircraft.
  • Not a promise of automatic failover. Path state is reported; automated failover is an engineering objective and will be described by measured behaviour.
