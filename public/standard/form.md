# Merge Combinator Form Agreement

**NOT WRITTEN.** This file is a placeholder so the set is visibly incomplete.

## Blocked on three decisions

The default splits by role cannot be drafted until these are answered. They are
PG's call and should not be invented.

1. **Does Merge contribute capital, or only the vehicle and the relationships?**
   Capital contribution materially changes the Studio position and changes whether
   this is a studio deal or an investor deal.
2. **Is the Technical Founder full time from day one, or converting later?**
   Sizing the largest founder position depends on when the clock starts.
3. **Does the venture expect an institutional round?** This sets how much room the
   cap table needs to leave and whether a standard option pool is carved at
   formation.

## What this document will contain

- Default splits by role, published as a table, with the ranges rather than a
  single number where the three variables above move them.
- Standard vesting. Four year with a one year cliff is the assumed default until
  decided otherwise.
- Spin-out terms: what happens to IP developed during Pursue, and on what terms it
  moves into the new entity.
- The Studio position and what, if anything, converts it to passive.
- The succession check: Merge's fractional seat has a permanent holder before
  Gate 4 to 5 passes.
- Founder departure and repurchase terms.
- IP assignment into the entity.

## Sequencing note

Publishing these terms in advance is what lets the Pursue Agreement avoid
promising to negotiate later, which is the failure mode that makes conventional
teaming agreements unenforceable. Until this document exists, Pursue section 9 is
pointing at something that is not there yet. That is the highest-priority gap in
the set.
