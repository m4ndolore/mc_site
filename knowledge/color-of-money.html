Budget Doctrine

The Color of Money

Appropriations law decides what the government may buy from you long before any customer decides whether they want it.

— Read the colors first

Scroll
01
Categories

Money arrives pre-committed

Five colors, five legal purposes

Congress appropriates money in colors: RDT&E for development, procurement for production buys, O&M for operations and services, MILCON for construction, MILPERS for people. Each carries a legal purpose and a lifespan: O&M expires in one year, RDT&E in two, procurement in three. A program office that spends the wrong color on you commits an Antideficiency Act violation, so it will not, no matter how much the end user wants the capability.

02
The Clock

Today's budget was written two years ago

PPBE runs at FY-2

The PPBE cycle programs money roughly two years before it is spent. If your capability has no budget line today, the earliest deliberate money arrives two POM cycles out. Current-year wins come from money already colored for your use (reprogramming actions, existing lines with headroom, innovation funds built to spend fast) while you work with a program office to plant lines in the out-years. Founders who plan revenue against the fiscal calendar stop being surprised by it.

03
Mismatch

Operator demand can't recolor dollars

Wanting it and buying it are separate authorities

The most common founder failure in this market: an end user who loves the product, connected to a budget holder legally unable to buy it. O&M can sustain a service and cannot capitalize your hardware. RDT&E can fund your prototype and cannot buy unit ten. Before you commit to a pilot, ask which color pays for production. Silence in response predicts the pilot's afterlife with uncomfortable accuracy.

04
Documents

The J-books are your market research

Demand signals, published annually

R-1 and P-1 exhibits and their justification books name programs, dollar amounts, and out-year plans, in public documents published every budget cycle. Founders who read them stop guessing. A budget line growing forty percent across the FYDP is procurement gravity you can build against. A flat line names the strategic latency you will have to out-wait or route around. Every serious defense company has someone who reads the J-books the week they drop.

05
Structure

Match the ask to the color

Structure converts intent into contract

SBIR, OTA, and Phase III authorities exist to convert colors: RDT&E into funded prototypes, prototype success into sole-source production. Structure your offer to the money that already exists (a service subscription for O&M, a prototype effort for RDT&E, a Phase III vehicle for production dollars) and the same capability becomes buyable three different ways. The founders who win this market sell the contract structure along with the product.
