Overview

Sunnoo is an American clean energy company building utility-patented, grid-independent EV charging infrastructure and solar optimization technology designed for rapid deployment without permitting, trenching, or utility interconnect delays.

Sunnoo's flagship platform combines a 6.5kW bifacial solar array with a patented dual-axis, single-motor tracking turret and configurable (24–100 kWh) battery storage, delivering Level 2 charging via two NACS connectors wherever the grid is unavailable, overtaxed, or too slow to connect.

The patented tracking turret uses 360-degree dual-axis tracking to capture 50–62% more energy than fixed arrays, with software-driven gains adding another 8–12% daily solar capture, at lower operating energy than traditional dual-motor systems.

Go-to-Market: partnerships and deployment across higher education, municipalities, and commercial fleets, with manufacturing rooted in Las Cruces, New Mexico.

Dual-Use Potential: grid-independent, rapid-deployment power is directly relevant to disaster relief, remote operations, and other environments where fixed grid infrastructure is unavailable or compromised.

Team: led by Partner & CEO Chris Langathianos, driving company strategy, commercialization, and go-to-market partnerships.
